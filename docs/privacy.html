Fuse: One Tap Chain Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Summary

Fuse is an offline puzzle game. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress (scores, streaks, settings) is stored only on your device.

Data the game stores

  • Game progress and settings, saved locally on your device. They are never uploaded by us.
  • If you use Export in Settings, a backup code is created and shared only where you choose to send it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Google → Ads).

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game creates a text or image of your result and hands it to the Android share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app to share with.

Children

Fuse is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for the Google Play Families program.
